Generated by `scripts/event_schema.py` from 40 simulated matches. Every event has `id, t (s), period, minute, clock, type`;
most have `team (0 home, 1 away)`, `player` (id), and `x, y` (pitch metres, 105 x 68, home attacks +x), plus `end_x, end_y` where it applies.
